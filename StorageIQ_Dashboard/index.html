Method

what this system actually does — and what it doesn't claim

Forecast

ARIMA (AIC-selected order) ensembled with a 2-layer LSTM, weighted by inverse hold-out MAE. Validated against naive, seasonal-naive and ETS baselines with MASE + walk-forward CV — leakage-free by construction, tested in CI.

Risk

Daily failures ~ Poisson(λ·N) with Gamma-distributed λ for over-dispersion, λ drifting along the forecast path. VaR₉₅/CVaR₉₅ of cumulative failures answer a real question: how many spares to stock.

Limitations

Fleet-level aggregation discards per-drive SMART signal; failure clustering across days is not modeled; anomaly labels are unsupervised. These are known trade-offs, not oversights — see README.
